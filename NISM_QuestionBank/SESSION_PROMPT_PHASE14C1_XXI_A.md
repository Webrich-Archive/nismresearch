## Key Content Areas to Cover per Topic

### Topic 1: PMS Investments Foundation (35 Qs) — Chapter 1
- Investment vs savings vs speculation
- Investment objectives (growth, income, preservation)
- Required rate of return: risk-free rate + risk premium; CAPM basics
- Risk types: inflation, interest rate, business, market, liquidity, currency, reinvestment
- Investment universe: equity, debt, real assets, alternatives, cash equivalents
- Channels: direct, MFs, PMS, AIFs — comparative lens
- Life-cycle pattern of savings
- **Question mix:** 10% calc, 60% conceptual, 10% regulatory, 20% application

### Topic 2: PMS Securities Markets Introduction (25 Qs) — Chapter 2
- Primary vs secondary market
- IPO/FPO/OFS/rights/private placement
- Market participants: investors, issuers, intermediaries, regulators
- Roles: stock broker, RTA, depository, custodian, MB, stock exchange, clearing corporation
- **Question mix:** 0% calc, 60% conceptual, 30% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 3: PMS Equity Investing (50 Qs) — Chapter 3
- Equity as asset class, equity risks
- Diversification — systematic vs unsystematic
- Top-down vs bottom-up stock selection
- Intrinsic value: DDM, FCFE, P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDA
- Technical analysis basics: trends, support/resistance, moving averages, RSI, MACD
- **Include at least 8 calc questions** on DDM, P/E, intrinsic value
- **Question mix:** 25% calc, 45% conceptual, 10% regulatory, 20% application

### Topic 4: PMS Fixed Income Investing (25 Qs) — Chapter 4
- Bond characteristics: coupon, maturity, YTM, call/put features
- Credit ratings and determinants of safety
- Bond valuation: present value of cash flows
- Duration, modified duration, convexity; price volatility
- **Include at least 5 calc questions** on bond pricing, duration, YTM
- **Question mix:** 30% calc, 45% conceptual, 10% regulatory, 15% application

### Topic 5: PMS Derivatives (25 Qs) — Chapter 5
- Futures and options fundamentals: payoffs, moneyness, margin
- Index, stock, commodity, currency derivatives
- PMS use of derivatives — hedging, not speculation-heavy
- Regulatory limits on derivatives in PMS
- **Include at least 5 calc questions** on option payoffs, futures P&L
- **Question mix:** 30% calc, 50% conceptual, 10% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 6: PMS Collective Investment Vehicles (25 Qs) — Chapter 6
- MF types (equity, debt, hybrid, ELSS), open vs close-ended, active vs passive
- REIT and InvIT structure — sponsor, manager, trustee, units
- AIF categories I/II/III — overlap with XIX-A and XIX-B content; focus PMS angle
- Legal structure comparison across MF, REIT, InvIT, AIF
- **Question mix:** 5% calc, 55% conceptual, 30% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 7: PMS Role of Portfolio Managers (50 Qs) — Chapter 7
- Overview of PMS in India (market size, SEBI regulatory framework)
- Discretionary vs Non-discretionary vs Advisory PMS
- Organizational structure: Principal Officer, Compliance Officer, Fund Managers, Dealers, Back office
- General responsibilities: suitability, risk profiling, periodic reporting
- Administration of investor portfolio: segregation of assets, demat accounts
- **Question mix:** 0% calc, 65% conceptual, 25% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 8: PMS Operational Aspects (65 Qs) — Chapter 8
Read Ch 8 (pages 101-120). Key areas:
- Eligible investors — Rs. 50 lakh minimum, Accredited Investors carve-out
- Mandatory disclosures to prospective clients (Disclosure Document contents)
- On-boarding: KYC, PAN, FATCA/CRS, risk profiling, client agreement, Direct Plan option
- Fresh commitments — segregated client funds/securities
- Liability in case of default
- Redressal of investor grievances — timelines, SCORES, SEBI reporting
- Disclosures to regulator — periodic filings, CTR-equivalent for PMS
- Costs/expenses/fees: management fee, performance fee (hurdle, HWM), exit load, fund expenses
- Performance reporting: time-weighted returns, pre/post-tax, client account statements
- **Include at least 8 calc questions** on fees and returns
- **Question mix:** 15% calc, 30% conceptual, 45% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 9: PMS Portfolio Management Process (75 Qs) — Chapter 9
Read Ch 9 carefully (pages 121-146) — largest chapter. Key areas:
- Importance of asset allocation decision
- Correlation across asset classes
- Portfolio management process steps: IPS, asset allocation, security selection, execution, monitoring, rebalancing
- Strategic vs tactical asset allocation
- Rebalancing: time-based, threshold-based, mixed
- Constraints: liquidity, time horizon, tax, legal/regulatory, unique circumstances
- Model portfolios in PMS
- **Include at least 15 calc questions** on allocation, correlation, portfolio expected return, variance
- **Question mix:** 25% calc, 45% conceptual, 15% regulatory, 15% application

### Topic 10: PMS Performance Measurement (50 Qs) — Chapter 10
Read Ch 10 (pages 147-157). Key areas:
- Holding period return, time-weighted return (TWR), money-weighted return (MWR/IRR)
- Arithmetic vs geometric mean returns
- Risk-adjusted return: Sharpe, Treynor, Jensen's alpha, Information ratio, Sortino
- Benchmarking and peer group analysis
- Performance attribution — Brinson-Hood-Beebower (allocation, selection, interaction)
- Valuation of securities by PM (listed, unlisted, illiquid)
- Portfolio Manager selection / due diligence
- **Include at least 12 calc questions** on Sharpe, Treynor, TWR, MWR, alpha
- **Question mix:** 35% calc, 40% conceptual, 10% regulatory, 15% application

### Topic 11: PMS Taxation (25 Qs) — Chapter 11
Read Ch 11 (pages 158-168). Key areas:
- Categories of investors (individuals, HUFs, firms, companies, trusts, NRIs)
- Tax treatment of equity (STCG 20% / LTCG 12.5% on Rs. 1.25 lakh, post July 2024), debt, REITs, InvITs, MF units
- Deductibility of PMS fees (generally NOT deductible against capital gains — Section 14A)
- Tax reporting and compliance: Form 16A, capital gain statements
- Portfolio Investment Scheme (PIS) for NRIs — RBI framework
- **Include at least 5 calc questions** on tax computation across asset classes
- **Question mix:** 25% calc, 35% conceptual, 30% regulatory, 10% application

### Topic 12: PMS Regulatory and Governance (50 Qs) — Chapter 12
Read Ch 12 (pages 169-182). Key areas:
- PMLA 2002 — CTR/STR thresholds, UBO identification, record retention (5 years)
- SEBI (PIT) Regulations 2015 — insider, connected person, UPSI, Code of Conduct, pre-clearance
- SEBI (PFUTP) Regulations 2003 — fraudulent/unfair practices definitions
- SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations 2020 — registration, capital requirements, investment limits, Disclosure Document, performance benchmarking framework, Direct Plan
- Code of Conduct for PMS Distributors — prohibited practices, disclosure of commissions
- Investor Charter for PMS
- **Question mix:** 5% calc, 35% conceptual, 55% regulatory, 5% application

## CRITICAL: Overlap Management with Prior Exams

PMS Distributors overlaps with several earlier NISM exams. **Do NOT duplicate existing questions verbatim.** Specifically:

1. **Ch 1-2 (Investments, Securities Markets):** Large overlap with Series V-A (MF), V-B (MF Foundation), XII (SM Foundation). For XXI-A, frame questions around how a PMS distributor applies these concepts — e.g., risk profiling for a Rs. 50 lakh+ PMS client.

2. **Ch 3 (Stocks):** Overlap with Series XV (Research Analyst) on fundamental/technical analysis. Focus XXI-A on stock selection for PMS portfolios, not deep RA-style models.

3. **Ch 4 (Fixed Income):** Overlap with Series IV (IRD). Focus XXI-A on bond choices within PMS portfolios — duration management, credit quality.

4. **Ch 5 (Derivatives):** Overlap with Series VIII (Equity Derivatives), I (Currency), IV (IRD). For XXI-A, restrict to PMS-specific derivative use (hedging-focused).

5. **Ch 6 (Collective Investment Vehicles):** Overlap with V-A, XIX-A, XIX-B. Frame from PMS-distributor viewpoint — comparing PMS vs MF vs AIF for specific client profiles.

6. **Ch 11 (Taxation):** Overlap with X-B (IA L2), XIX-A/B. For XXI-A, focus on taxation as it flows to PMS clients' statements (since PMS is pass-through at SEBI level, each transaction taxed individually).

7. **Ch 12 (Regulatory):** Overlap with XXIV (AML), III-A/C (Compliance), XIX-B (Cat III). Unique to XXI-A: **SEBI (Portfolio Managers) Regulations 2020** — ensure full coverage of PM-specific provisions.

8. **Unique XXI-A content (no significant overlap):**
   - PMS organizational structure (Principal Officer, Compliance Officer roles)
   - On-boarding process for Rs. 50 lakh+ investors
   - Performance benchmarking under SEBI (PM) Regulations 2020
   - PMS fees model (fixed + performance fee, no mutual-fund-style TER cap)
   - Section 14A — PMS fees not deductible against capital gains
   - PIS (Portfolio Investment Scheme) for NRIs under FEMA
